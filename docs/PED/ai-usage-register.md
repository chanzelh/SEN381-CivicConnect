## Purpose

This register records AI-assisted material and contributions used across the CivicConnect project, including the human verification performed, the decision taken and any issues found. AI assistance does not replace human review or establish complete test coverage or security.

Dates use `DD/MM/YYYY`. Entries are listed chronologically. Decision values are Accepted, Modified or Rejected. Blank historical issue fields are recorded as “Not recorded” rather than treated as evidence that no issues occurred.
