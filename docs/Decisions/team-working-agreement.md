## Review Expectations
Turnaround expectation for PR review: Same day reviews/acceptance is expected. If there are disagreements they should be resolved same day.

## Conflict Resolution
Process: If there is a conflict within the team, it will be resolved through a meeting on Discord to ensure it can be sorted out effectively instead of only relying on messages. The team will strive to have effective communication and teamwork to ensure conflicts don't occur unnecessarily

## Missed Deadlines
Process: The first step would be to figure out what the missed deadline was due to (eg. if it was due to too much work, not understanding it etc). Then once that is found, the other two team members can offer help with the work to ensure the group does not fall behind.

## AI Usage
Per use of AI, it needs to be logged into the AI Usage Register to ensure continuous trackkeeping of each member's usage.
