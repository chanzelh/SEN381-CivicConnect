## Purpose

The purpose of this register is to identify, assess and track engineering risks that may affect the CivicConnect project. These risks may relate to areas such as requirements, security, schedule, collaboration, quality, dependencies, integration, project scope and the use of AI.

Each risk is assessed according to its probability and potential impact. Mitigation actions describe how the team will reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk, while contingency actions describe what the team will do if the risk occurs.

The risk register will be reviewed and updated throughout the project as new risks are identified or existing risks change.
